Building Things That Matter

Evergreen Created 16 Feb 2026 Last tended 8 Oct 2026

Beginnings are hard. So is building things that actually work at scale.

For years, my writing has lived in private: design docs, post-mortems, and “decision records” meant for internal eyes only. These are the documents that move millions of dollars and affect real systems. But lately, I've noticed a pattern. Whether I'm looking at payment gateways, distributed systems, or the current wave of AI, the same fundamental challenges keep appearing.

I'm starting this Substack because I want to bridge the gap between high-level “fintech/AI hype” and the less-glamorous, sometimes ambiguous, oft technical reality of how money actually moves, and how systems scale.

Over the last year, I've been deep in the plumbing — Payment models, Merchant of Record (MOR) infrastructure, reconciliation loops, and the high-stakes world of financial infrastructure. It's a space where a “minor” architectural choice can quietly bleed revenue or destroy customer trust six months down the line.

I'm starting this Substack because I'm currently in the middle of that “deep dive.” I'm connecting my decade of engineering leadership with the high-stakes plumbing of global financial infrastructure models.

At the same time, AI is changing how we operate. It's not about the “magic” of LLMs; it's about how we use these tools to compress the massive complexity of the domains we already work in.

I plan to publish 1–2 times per week. Most of my writing will be free and public. I'll be focusing on:

  • Payments Infrastructure: Deconstructing how money actually moves.
  • The “Outsider” Perspective: Applying distributed systems logic to legacy financial plumbing, and beyond.
  • Applied AI: How I'm using AI to handle financial modeling and complexity of monies.

If you are a builder, an operator, or a leader who cares about how systems actually behave in production, you'll feel at home here.
